## AI as a system

Artificial intelligence is not only about models. It includes data, goals, evaluation, human context, deployment, and product design.

## Topics I am connecting

I am studying machine learning, neural networks, deep learning, transformers, healthcare AI, and data science programming.

- Models need good data.
- Evaluation needs realistic tasks.
- Products need clear user value.

## Why it matters

The more I learn, the more I see AI as a practical layer in business systems, automation, education, and digital products.
