## Start with the workflow

An AI product should begin with a real job someone is already trying to do. The model is not the product by itself. The product is the improved workflow.

## What makes AI useful

Useful AI systems reduce friction, save time, improve clarity, or unlock a capability that was too expensive before.

- Define the user and the decision.
- Decide what the AI should automate, assist, or explain.
- Build fallbacks for uncertainty.

## Founder lens

The best AI products are not only technically interesting. They are trustworthy, specific, and easy to adopt.
