## Mobility is coordination

A mobility platform is not only an app. It is a coordination system between customers, drivers, routes, vehicles, pricing, support, and operations.

## Where intelligence helps

AI can support dispatch, demand prediction, onboarding checks, route planning, support triage, and operational dashboards.

- Dispatch should balance speed, fairness, and reliability.
- Drivers need clear onboarding and transparent workflows.
- Operators need visibility into exceptions.

## Forare lens

Forare can become valuable by focusing on the driver and operator workflow first, then using intelligence to make the system smoother over time.
