## Daily Article Selection Rule

Use the earliest `planned` article whose topic still makes sense based on recently published posts. If a new timely topic appears, it can replace a planned article only when it fits the same pillar balance and service funnel.

## Balance Rule

Each 14-day period should include:

- 3 to 4 AI automation articles.
- 2 to 3 applied AI learning articles.
- 2 AI project leadership articles.
- 1 to 2 founder/operator notes.
- 1 responsible AI or healthcare article.
- 1 project/platform article.
- 1 personal-brand or content-system article.
